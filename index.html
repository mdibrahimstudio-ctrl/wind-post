★★★
How to play
✈️ Paper planes launch from the left and glide. Gravity pulls them down, but speed gives them lift.
🌬 Drag with mouse or finger to draw a wind-gust line. A plane near the line is pushed along it. Gusts fade after 3 seconds.
📦 Steer the plane into the glowing red mailbox. Fly through golden rings for bonus stars.
⚡ Fans blow all the time. Avoid the ground, water and storm clouds — a hard hit loses a plane. You get 3 planes per level.
💡 Gentle ground touches bounce. Watch the Wind Ink meter — it refills slowly.

Progress is saved on this device. Sound: tiny bleeps only.
