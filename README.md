# Wind Post

A 2D browser game built with Google Gemini Canvas. Deliver paper planes carrying letters to mailboxes by drawing wind gusts.

## How to play

- Paper planes launch from the left and glide with gravity and lift.
- Drag with mouse or touch to draw a wind-gust line; planes inside a gust are pushed along it.
- Guide the plane into the glowing mailbox. Fly through golden rings for bonus stars.
- Avoid the ground, water and storm clouds. 3 planes per level, 12 levels.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or play the deployed version on Vercel.
